# DISTINCTION
## A Short Film on Taste and the Camera

*After Bourdieu*

---

**Runtime:** ~4 minutes

---

### SCENE 1: THE HABITUS

**[OPEN ON: M2U00248.MPG @ 0:00]**
*Flamingos standing in water. Pink. Ornamental.*

**V/O:**
Pierre Bourdieu spent his life studying one question: Why do we like what we like?

**[CUT TO: M2U00248.MPG @ 0:20]**
*A flamingo bends to feed. Elegant. Graceful.*

**V/O:**
His answer was uncomfortable. We do not *choose* our tastes. They are given to us. By our class. Our education. Our parents.

**[CUT TO: M2U00284.MPG @ 0:00-0:07]**
*A white bird in an enclosure. Less elegant. Caged.*

**V/O:**
He called this the *habitus*—the invisible structure that shapes what we find beautiful. What we find... worth filming.

---

### SCENE 2: CULTURAL CAPITAL

**[CUT TO: M2U00227.MPG @ 0:30-0:35]**
*A hand holds up Wittgenstein and Lyotard books.*

**V/O:**
Some people film philosophy books.

**[CUT TO: M2U00227.MPG @ 0:35-0:50]**
*The Postmodern Condition displayed to camera.*

**V/O:**
This is *cultural capital*—the accumulated knowledge that marks you as educated. Sophisticated. Worth listening to.

**[CUT TO: M2U00541.MPG @ 0:00]**
*A movie theater entrance. Bright. Commercial.*

**V/O:**
Others film marquees.

**[CUT TO: M2U00541.MPG @ 0:10]**
*Movie posters. Mainstream entertainment.*

**V/O:**
Neither is more *true*. But they signal very different positions in the social field.

---

### SCENE 3: THE BODY AND THE LENS

**[CUT TO: M2U00276.MPG @ 0:22-0:25]**
*Woman notices camera. Discomfort.*

**V/O:**
Bourdieu understood that the body knows things the mind does not.

**[CUT TO: M2U00276.MPG @ 0:30-0:32]**
*She turns away.*

**V/O:**
This woman's body understands what it means to be filmed. The extraction. The power asymmetry.

**[CUT TO: M2U00279.MPG @ 0:17-0:20]**
*A man looks at camera. Then away.*

**V/O:**
It takes cultural capital to be *comfortable* with the gaze. To perform for it.

**[CUT TO: M2U00243.MPG @ 0:47-0:50]**
*A friend smiles at camera. Easy. Relaxed.*

**V/O:**
See the difference?

**[CUT TO: M2U00243.MPG @ 0:23]**
*Friends posing together. Practiced.*

**V/O:**
These people know how to be seen. It is a skill. And like all skills, it is unevenly distributed.

---

### SCENE 4: SYMBOLIC VIOLENCE

**[CUT TO: M2U00256.MPG @ 0:05]**
*The snake in its enclosure. Watched.*

**V/O:**
Bourdieu called it *symbolic violence*—the soft coercion that makes domination feel natural.

**[CUT TO: M2U00247.MPG @ 0:20-0:30]**
*The swan resting. Peaceful. Unaware.*

**V/O:**
The zoo animal does not resist because it does not know resistance is possible.

**[CUT TO: M2U00268.MPG @ 0:18]**
*Woman actively denying the camera.*

**V/O:**
But she does.

**[CUT TO: M2U00227.MPG @ 3:05-3:10]**
*The driver puts on the ski mask.*

**V/O:**
And so does he.

---

### SCENE 5: THE FIELD

**[CUT TO: M2U00637.MPG @ 0:13]**
*A band on stage. Lights. Performance.*

**V/O:**
Bourdieu said we all exist in *fields*—invisible arenas of competition. Art. Music. Academia.

**[CUT TO: M2U00637.MPG @ 1:01-1:18]**
*The drummer. The audience. The apparatus visible.*

**V/O:**
In each field, we struggle for position. For recognition. For the right to define what counts as valuable.

**[CUT TO: M2U00757.MPG @ 0:04-0:17]**
*Lens flares. Abstract light. Night.*

**V/O:**
And now there is a new field. The largest one ever created.

**[CUT TO: M2U00226.MPG @ 0:00-0:05]**
*Car infotainment screen. Glowing.*

**V/O:**
The field of images.

---

### SCENE 6: CODA

**[CUT TO: M2U00227.MPG @ 5:20-5:25]**
*Masked driver. Blue light.*

**V/O:**
In this field, we are all players. All subjects. All objects.

**[CUT TO: M2U00252.MPG @ 1:05]**
*Snake eyes. Returned gaze.*

**V/O:**
The question Bourdieu would ask: Who taught you how to hold the camera?

**[CUT TO: M2U00243.MPG @ 0:51-0:53]**
*The act of filming made visible.*

**V/O:**
And who taught you how to smile when it points at you?

**[FADE TO BLACK]**

**V/O:**
These are not natural abilities. They are forms of capital.

**[HOLD ON BLACK]**

**V/O:**
And capital is never distributed equally.
